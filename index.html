帮助

怎么做一颗自己的骰子？

点底部的「创建」，选「文字选项」，用逗号隔开写几条（比如「拉面,咖喱,火锅」），写几条就是几面。也可以选「数字」做 2 到 100 面的数字骰。按「保存」就上桌了，同时存进仓库。

怎么改或删掉一颗骰子？

点桌上的骰子打开它的编辑页。想从桌上拿走，按住骰子拖进右下角的垃圾桶。仓库里的骰子点卡片右上角的笔进编辑，可以彻底删除。

字太长会怎样？

原文照样刻在骰面上。只掷一颗时，骰子停稳后镜头会推近那一面；掷好几颗时，面板上会写出每颗的原文。

Plus 包含什么？怎么恢复购买？

Plus 是一次性买断（不是订阅）：仓库不限条数、桌上最多 8 颗、全部皮肤和桌面，以后新加的也都包含。换了手机的话，在「设置」里点「恢复购买」。

声音和震动

在「设置」里可以分别关掉。手机静音时掷骰没有声音。

Help

How do I make my own die?

Tap Create at the bottom, choose Text, and write a few options separated by commas (e.g. “Ramen, Curry, Pizza”). Each option becomes a face. You can also choose Numbers for a 2- to 100-sided numeric die. Tap Save and it goes onto the table and into your library.

How do I edit or remove a die?

Tap a die on the table to edit it. To take it off the table, drag it into the trash at the bottom right. In the library, tap the pencil on a card to edit or permanently delete it.

What happens with long text?

The full text is always printed on the faces. With a single die, the camera zooms in on the result face after it settles; with several dice, the panel shows each die’s result in full.

What does Plus include? How do I restore it?

Plus is a one-time purchase, not a subscription: unlimited library, up to 8 dice on the table, and every skin and table, including ones added later. On a new phone, open Settings and tap Restore Purchase.

Sounds and haptics

You can turn each off in Settings. Roll sounds are muted when your phone is on silent.
